03 — Multi-Dimensional AI

What Does UrbanLens See?

AI-powered visual analysis across multiple critical urban dimensions.

Green & Environmental

Detect and evaluate vegetation, tree canopy, green spaces, sky visibility, and environmental appearance.

  • Vegetation density mapping
  • Tree canopy coverage
  • Sky-view factor calculation

Pedestrian Experience

Analyze sidewalk presence, walkable space, pedestrian accessibility, street obstruction, and human scale.

  • Sidewalk continuity index
  • Barrier & obstruction detection
  • Human-scale friendliness

Built Environment

Evaluate building façades, architectural condition, building density, diversity, and street enclosure.

  • Façade condition scoring
  • Architectural diversity index
  • Street enclosure ratio

Streetscape

Identify roads, street furniture, signage, lighting, parking facilities, and utility infrastructure.

  • Street furniture inventory
  • Lighting adequacy assessment
  • Signage clutter detection

Cleanliness & Maintenance

Assess visual indicators such as litter, road surface condition, infrastructure maintenance quality, and visual clutter.

  • Litter & debris detection
  • Pavement cracking & wear
  • Graffiti & wear tracking

Visual Character

Understand color composition, visual complexity, streetscape coherence, overall attractiveness, and urban identity.

  • Color harmony analysis
  • Visual complexity metric
  • Coherence scoring
